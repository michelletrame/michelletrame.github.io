About

The person behind the work.

Hi, I'm Michelle, a product designer and leader working on Canvas at Instructure.

  1. Over 10 years in design, on a career path that includes outdoor leadership and cartography (the original UX design discipline). These experiences inform how I show up as a teammate, communicator and leader: how I build trust, break down complicated problems and collaborate.
  2. From global enterprises to early-stage startups to a stint as an entrepreneur with a successful exit, I've shipped innovative products with real reach for global companies and defined the way forward for 0-to-1 products at startups like Vouch and In the Test. Whether the lens is UX design, product design or service design, my mindset is meeting the moment while still considering scope and scale.
  3. Drawn to hard problems and mission-driven teams. I do my best work bringing order (and joy!) to messy, high-stakes systems. I've led design teams and cross-functional teams of two to twenty, revitalizing internal systems and ways of working with the same iterative approach that works for building products.

My design ethos

Catching falling fruit.

{% include img.html src="about/lemons" alt="A cluster of ripe yellow lemons hanging among dark green leaves on a lemon tree." sizes="(min-width: 900px) 320px, 60vw" class="ethos-photo" %}

Collaboration is at the core of my values. In my early twenties, living in a Santa Cruz house with probably too many people and even more bikes, I met a group who were gleaning: collecting fruit from trees in the medians of busy boulevards, in front yards and in tiny backyard orchards.

The goal was to intercept food (with permission) that would otherwise go to waste and turn it into something delicious, nourishing and useful. We shared the fresh fruit with friends and food banks, and made ciders, preserves and fruit butters. It felt radical.

It opened my eyes to how much abundance is around us that we fail to use. As a designer, I aim to capture the goodness that is right at hand. Whether in small details or complex systems, I work in the space of things beyond our notice, or that we haven't yet put to use, to create enduringly joyful products, services and experiences.
