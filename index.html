Designing for
joyful moments.

Hi, I'm Michelle. I'm a collaboration-loving, research-oriented, AI-confident product designer and leader working on Canvas, the learning platform at the center of classrooms in more than 100 countries.

I'm drawn to the problems that look diabolical at first: messy, complex systems and environments. I go deep, reframe to find root causes, and design backward from the outcomes that matter to the humans trying to get things done, until every workflow, interaction and touchpoint feels simple and joyful.
