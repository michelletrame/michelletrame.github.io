Designing for
joyful moments.

Hi, I'm Michelle. I'm a collaboration-loving, research-oriented product designer and leader working on Canvas, a pillar of edtech with over 30 million active users.

As designers we are often concerned with discovering pain points and implementing solutions to address those. I am just as concerned with exploring joyful moments and happy places so that I can design not just for alleviating discomfort, but for maximizing happiness.

Catching falling fruit.

In Santa Cruz I joined a group gleaning fruit from median trees and backyard orchards, food that would otherwise go to waste. It opened my eyes to how much abundance is around us. As a designer, I try to capture the goodness that's right at hand.
