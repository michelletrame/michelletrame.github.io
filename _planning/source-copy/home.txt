h1: Hi, I'm Michelle.
h1: I'm a collaboration-loving, research-oriented product designer working on Canvas. Formerly a team member designing employee tools at REI Co-op and on the innovation team at AT&T's in-house design studio. As designers we are often concerned with discovering pain points and implementing solutions to address those. I am just as concerned with exploring joyful moments and happy places so that I can design not just for alleviating discomfort, but for maximizing happiness. This is some of my work. ‍
