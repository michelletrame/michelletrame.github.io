h1: Making teaching tools work for teachers
div: CANVAS
div: The Challenge
div: Teachers want ways to sort and organize students in their classes for differentiated learning.
div: Our Solution
div: A lightweight and a full-featured implementation of the tool. The lightweight version allowed us to go to market quickly while gathering more information about the size of the demand for the full-featured option.
div: My Contributions
div: I was the lead designer for all work related to building this feature, including preliminary surveys, generative research, content design, user testing and creation of all relevant design files and specs.
