The feature

Tags that only teachers can see

A differentiation tag is a way of sorting students into groups. Unlike Canvas's collaborative groups, tags aren't visible to students and aren't meant for collaboration.

  • Create and manage tags from the People page, either as a single tag or as a tag set with several variants (for example, reading groups).
  • Tag students in bulk by selecting them on the People page and choosing Tag As; the same control removes a tag.
  • Assign content to a tag: modules, quizzes, pages, discussions and assignments can go to individual students, sections or tags. Students who aren't assigned don't see the item.
